The Problem

A normal hike can become dangerous gradually.

It rarely starts with a fall. It starts with small, easy-to-ignore shifts — until the options run out.

Moving slower→ Weather worsens→ Less daylight left→ Battery draining→ Signal disappears
Most apps show a map, or offer an SOS button — only after something has already gone wrong. PeakGuard tries to spot the dangerous trend before that happens.
How PeakGuard Works

Four steps, before something goes wrong

1

Plan

You choose a route, a start time, and your experience level.

2

Analyze

PeakGuard evaluates route difficulty, elevation, weather, daylight, your experience and expected duration.

3

Monitor

On the trail, it tracks GPS, pace, route deviation, remaining time, battery, and the Decision Point.

4

Act

If risk rises, PeakGuard doesn't just warn you — it tells you what to do.
